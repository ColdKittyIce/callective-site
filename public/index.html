    How you get on the phone

    The line

    Calls happen in the WOPC Discord voice channel, and the real queue lives there. Here is how to get on the phone, plus a practice line to mess around with.

    1. 1

      Jack into the WOPC Discord

      Hop in the voice channel called The Callective (Saturdays at 7pm EST). There's no separate Callective server.

    2. 2

      Drop a PLA bell

      Put the PLA bell emoji in the voice channel's text chat, or type /join. You're on the line.

    3. 3

      Wait for your ticket

      The host runs !next and the bot pings whoever's up.

    4. 4

      Make the call

      The host dials, you do the bit. On an All Call, the whole room piles onto one line.
